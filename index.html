About Antariksha

Space, without limits.

In Sanskrit, Antariksha means the cosmos — vastness, limitless possibility, the beauty of uncharted realms.

We bring that same expansive, imaginative thinking to every home and space we design, while staying grounded in one belief: good design should feel timeless, not trendy — shaped by transparency, collaboration, and a close read of how you actually live.

Aakanksha Gaikwad Founder & Interior Designer, Antariksha Interiors
